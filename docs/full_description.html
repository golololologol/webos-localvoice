Speech recognition that runs on the TV. Nothing you say leaves it.

Hold the remote's microphone button, speak and let go. Your words go into the selected text box, Kodi's keyboard or a search on YouTube, and apps with their own microphone button get them too. A card at the bottom of the screen shows how loud your voice is.

Speech models are downloaded in the app. Choose Parakeet TDT 110M (108 MB) or Moonshine tiny-en (30 MB) for English, or Phonon 2.5 (183 MB) for 25 European languages.

Local Voice replaces LG's voice service while it is on, and gives it back when you turn it off. It needs root. After an update, turn it off and on again.

Based on Local Voice for the LG BX with webOS 5.4, with microphone updates tested on LG C3 and C4 TVs.
